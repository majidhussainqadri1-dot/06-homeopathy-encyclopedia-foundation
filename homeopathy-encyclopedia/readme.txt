A searchable American English encyclopedia foundation for the Sabri Social Homeopathy Platform.

== Description ==

File 06 builds the first 25 percent of the Encyclopedia and knowledge-relationship system.

Features:
* Sixteen fixed knowledge types.
* American English public interface, starter content, forms, and administration.
* Search, A–Z browsing, type, body-system, latest, and popular filters.
* Structured entry fields, references, medical red flags, and safety limitations.
* Relationships to encyclopedia entries, learning books, and lessons.
* Founder entries publish immediately; verified doctor entries require review.
* Bookmarks, Saved Knowledge, corrections, reports, comments, and view counts.
* Encyclopedia Management, reviewer identity, audit history, and privacy hooks.
* DefinedTerm, Article, and MedicalWebPage structured data.

== Installation ==

1. Keep Files 01 through 05 active.
2. Upload this ZIP through WordPress Admin > Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin.
3. Activate Homeopathy Encyclopedia Foundation.
4. Open the central Encyclopedia page.
5. Use Encyclopedia Management for moderation, corrections, and reports.
6. Eligible authors may use the automatically created Submit Encyclopedia Entry page.

This plugin enhances the Foundation-managed Encyclopedia page without changing the active theme or WordPress Reading Settings.

== Editorial and medical notice ==

Every entry requires references. Health Condition and Pathology entries require medical red flags. Remedy entries require safety and limitations. Entries must not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee a cure, delay emergency care, or reproduce copyrighted material without permission.
